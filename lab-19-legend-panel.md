# Lab 19 — Legend Panel

## My company

I used NVDA, Nvidia Corporation, as the largest-company run for the global-equity sleeve (VT) in my Project 2 universe. It is a U.S. SEC filer, so the three skills can use the same five annual 10-K fact rows.

## Panel result

- Buffett: 3 of 3 checks pass.
- Cathie Wood: revenue growth and the latest R&D check pass.
- Burry: the latest liabilities-to-equity check passes, but operating cash flow was not at least net income every year.

## Bull and bear

Bull case: Buffett's three checks pass, and Cathie Wood's growth and R&D checks pass on the same five-year facts. Bear case: Burry's cash-conversion check fails, so rapid growth does not remove the need to inspect how earnings convert into cash.

## Decision

I would let the panel screen companies only with the condition that it remains a first-pass screen: every missing value stays missing, and any failed check must be reviewed in the underlying annual filing before an investment decision.
