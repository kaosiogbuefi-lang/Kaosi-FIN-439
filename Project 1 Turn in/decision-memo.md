# McDonald's (MCD) - Investment Committee Decision Memo

## Recommendation

**Watch/defer; do not initiate at $238.32 per share as of September 23, 2026.** My FCFF valuation is $168.70 to $198.15 per share, with a base case of $183.15. The reference market price is above the high FCFF case.

## Evidence and valuation range

| Method | Result | Use and limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| FCFF DCF | $168.70 / $183.15 / $198.15 low/base/high | Primary valuation. Based on explicit five-year cash-flow forecasts, a 7.05% WACC, 2.5% terminal growth, and an enterprise-to-equity bridge. |
| Peer P/E | $309.82-$388.96 | Qualified cross-check only. Small peer sample; QSR's discontinued operations reduce comparability. |
| Peer EV/EBITDA | $260.14-$302.49 | Qualified cross-check only. Provider EBITDA definitions require issuer-level reconciliation. |

The methods are not averaged. FCFF values operating cash flows and explicitly subtracts debt; P/E embeds each peer's capital structure and below-the-line earnings; EV/EBITDA depends on consistent EBITDA definitions.

## Key assumptions and sensitivity

- Revenue-growth path: 4.0%, 4.0%, 3.5%, 3.0%, and 2.5% from FY2026-FY2030.
- WACC: 7.05%.
- Terminal growth: 2.5%.
- Downside case: reducing every forecast-year growth rate by 1.0 percentage point produces $168.70 per share.
- Terminal value: 81.3% of enterprise value, so long-run assumptions are a material limitation.
- Reverse DCF: matching the $238.32 market price requires adding 3.51 percentage points to every explicit annual growth assumption while holding other base assumptions fixed.

## Risks and monitoring triggers

The main risks are weaker comparable sales, slower system-unit growth, lower franchise/company-operated margins, higher capex, higher interest expense or debt, and a higher supportable WACC. I would monitor comparable sales, unit growth, operating margin, capex guidance, interest expense, debt, and cash policy.

## Committee ask

Do not initiate a position now. Reconsider if the price falls below a refreshed conservative FCFF range with a sufficient margin of safety, or if primary evidence supports a sustained improvement in growth, margins, capex, or discount-rate assumptions.
