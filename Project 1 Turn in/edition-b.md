# Project 1 - Edition B: McDonald's Corporation (MCD)

## Committee decision

**Watch-defer; do not initiate MCD at the September 23, 2026 reference price of $238.32 per share.** The FCFF DCF produces a base value of $183.15 per diluted share and a $168.70 to $198.15 low/base/high range. The market price is above the high FCFF case.

## What changed from Edition A

Edition A was a preliminary watch-defer position because intrinsic value, a current market-price comparison, and a current share-count analysis were incomplete. Edition B adds a five-year FCFF model, enterprise-to-equity bridge, sensitivity analysis, reverse DCF, peer cross-checks, and validation checks. The direction of the recommendation did not change, but the evidence is now more specific: the FCFF range is below the reference market price, while peer methods disagree and remain qualified rather than averaged.

## Material assumptions and limitations

The base forecast revenue-growth path is 4.0%, 4.0%, 3.5%, 3.0%, and 2.5%. The core limitations are WACC, terminal growth, capex, gross-margin treatment, excess cash, debt treatment, and terminal-value dependence. The model should be updated if primary evidence changes comparable sales, system-unit growth, operating margin, capex guidance, interest expense, debt, or cash policy.

## Peer cross-checks

Peer P/E suggests $309.82 to $388.96 per share and peer EV/EBITDA suggests $260.14 to $302.49 per share. These results are cross-checks, not inputs to an average. P/E includes peer capital structures and below-the-line earnings; EV/EBITDA depends on comparable EBITDA definitions; FCFF values operating cash flow and then explicitly handles cash and debt. QSR's discontinued-operations issue and unreconciled peer EBITDA definitions reduce the weight placed on the peer methods.

## Conditional reversal

Reconsider an investment if the market price falls below a refreshed conservative FCFF range with a sufficient margin of safety, or if primary evidence supports sustainably stronger growth or margins, lower capex needs, or a lower supportable WACC.
