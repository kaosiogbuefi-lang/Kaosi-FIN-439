Hello. This is Video 1 for my Project 1 analysis of McDonald's. I am evaluating whether a long-only investment committee should initiate a position in McDonald's.

This first file is my unchanged Edition A. My starting position was watch-defer. At that point, I thought McDonald's looked like a strong, cash-generating company, but I did not have the valuation completed yet. Now I am switching to my Edition B file. My final recommendation is still watch-defer, but it is more specific. My reference date is September 23, 2026. At that time, MCD traded at $238.32, and my FCFF model gives a range of about $168.70 to $198.15 per share, which is below that price. Therefore, I would not initiate a position at that reference price.

The main change from Edition A to Edition B came from course instruction, my own analysis, and AI-assisted organization. I built a five-year FCFF model, added sensitivity and reverse-DCF analysis, and used peer P/E and EV/EBITDA as cross-checks.

Next, I am moving to the research-evolution Markdown file. This file shows the changes in the table. One suggestion I received from AI was to use peer valuation beside my DCF. I checked that suggestion and qualified it instead of fully accepting it. P/E and FCFF use different valuation objects and capital-structure conventions. Also, the P/E group is small, and QSR, one of the peers, has discontinued-operations issues. Because of that, I show the peer results separately and do not average them into my DCF.

Lastly, the evidence that would change my view would be stronger future comparable sales, unit growth, margins, and lower capital spending. A lower market price could also create a sufficient margin of safety.
