# Sources

## Prior 10-Ks — the same company page

[SEC EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) → your ticker → form type `10-K` → open the **two years before** the one you already have.

What did management promise then, and did it happen?

## Recent news — from the company itself

Web-search `[company name] investor relations` → **News** or **Press releases**.

Use dated, attributable company releases that are easy to cite.

## CEO interview or earnings call

Use the same investor-relations page → **Events** or **Quarterly results** → the call replay or transcript.

Alternatively, search YouTube for `[company name] CEO interview`.
