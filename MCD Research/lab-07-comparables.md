## Interpretation

Removing Group 1, the higher-P/E peer, lowers the remaining median-implied price to $215.81. With only one peer remaining there is a reference estimate, not a range; the calculation does not prove Asbury is fairly valued because business quality, growth, risk, and earnings comparability can differ.
